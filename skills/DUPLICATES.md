## Shared startup and long procedures

Gstack's repeated startup protocol is split into deduplicated section references in core/gstack-operating-protocol. Skill-name launch parameters are explicitly parameterized. Source-specific startup, checkpoint, telemetry, and onboarding code is retained only as reference, never activated automatically. Long methodologies move intact to a required reference and remain indexed by their entrypoints.

Conflicting prescriptions are enumerated with composition rules in [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md). Machine-readable duplicate decisions cover exact and same-name candidates; the domain table records broader semantic overlaps. These are curated normalization decisions, not a claim of formal semantic equivalence between every pair of documents.
